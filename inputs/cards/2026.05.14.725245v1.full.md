# Overview of the DamageFormer architecture

**Authors:** Qiang Yang, Lin Li, Qin Ma, Rui Yin
**Year:** 2026
**DOI:** 10.1101/2026.05.14.725245

## Abstract

Background: DNA lesions arise from endogenous metabolism and environmental exposure and are the major drivers of mutagenesis, aging, and cancer development. However, mapping DNA damage at nucleotide resolution remains a technically challenging task. Nanopore sequencing enables direct detection of chemical perturbations through alterations in ionic current signals. Despite this potential, existing computational approaches remain limited in their capacity to generalize across diverse lesion types and to effectively integrate nucleotide sequence context with raw signal information for accurate detection and localization.
