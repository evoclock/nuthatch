Although contamination can 13 - affect responses and covariates, we focus on response contamination and evaluate 14 - Random Forests through simulation. Using a synthetic animal-breeding dataset, we 15 - assess robust Random Forests across several contamination scenarios and validate them 16 - on plant and animal datasets. We thereby clarify the consequences of contamination for 17 - prediction, develop a robust Random Forest framework, and evaluate its performance. 18 - We examine preprocessing or data-transformation strategies, algorithmic modifications, 19 - and hybrid approaches for robustifying Random Forests. Across these approaches, data 20 - transformation emerges as the most effective strategy, delivering the strongest 21 - performance under contamination. This strategy is simple, general, and transferable to 22 - other Machine Learning methods, offering a remedy for robust genomic prediction.
